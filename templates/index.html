About Learnova

Learnova is an AI-powered study assistant built to help students learn faster and understand better. Instead of switching between different tools, students can ask questions, summarize notes and test themselves with quizzes — all in one place.

Features

Chat with

Ask any study question and get clear, step-by-step answers.

Notes Summarizer

Turn long notes into short summaries and key points.

Quiz Generator

Create practice quizzes from any topic and test yourself.

Study Goals & Streaks

Set a daily goal, track your study time and build a streak.
